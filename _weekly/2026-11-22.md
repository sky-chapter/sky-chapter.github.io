# Sky Chapter · Weekly — “Full Moon, New Horizon”

*Week of November 16–22, 2026 · recap & what's next · reflection & entertainment, not prediction*

## Recap
- **Courage got lucky.** [Mars and Jupiter](/signs/leo) held hands in Leo all week (conjunction, orb ~1¾° → 2½°) — nerve with a tailwind. One bold ask fit.
- **Gas pedal met brake, then ground.** The Moon ran through Aries into a retrograde [Saturn](/notes/retrograde) embrace (Day 1), stood exactly opposite [Venus](/signs/libra) on an almost-full wire (Day 2, orb 0°09′), then landed in Taurus as the season turned (Day 3). Our nightly question travelled with it: *motion vs progress → autopilot → what am I ready to leave behind?*
- **The brake trio held the gates.** Saturn, Uranus and Neptune all retrograde, Mercury burrowing into Scorpio — a week for rewrites, not launches.
- **The season flipped.** On Sunday the Sun crossed Scorpio → Sagittarius (0°42′). Depth gave way to distance; the year's last big horizon opened.

- The Moon fades from full toward last quarter (≈64% lit by Nov 30): a week for finishing, not starting. Words run hot at both edges (Moon opposite Mercury Nov 23, square Nov 30) — reread before you send.
