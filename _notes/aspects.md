# Aspects

An **aspect** is the angle between two planets — how their stories meet.

- **Conjunction (0°)** — they stand together; merged energy.
- **Sextile (60°)** — an opening; cooperation needs a nudge.
- **Square (90°)** — friction; the belt that checks your mirrors.
- **Trine (120°)** — flow; luck on tap.
- **Opposition (180°)** — across-the-table; tug-of-war or balance.

**Orb** — the allowed gap from the exact angle; we accept **6–8°**, so nothing gets noisy.
