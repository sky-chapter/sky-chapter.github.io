# Orb

The **orb** is how far an aspect may sit from its perfect angle (0°, 60°, 90°, 120°, 180°) and still count. We keep **6°** for most planets and **8°** involving the Sun or Moon — tight enough to stay meaningful, loose enough to be honest about measurement.

Example: “Moon–Venus opposition, orb 0°09′” = almost exact — a strong, clear signal.
