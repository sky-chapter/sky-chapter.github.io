# Houses

A **house** is one of twelve life areas (self, money, home, career…). In Sky Chapter we use **whole-sign solar houses**: your Sun sign is house 1, and the next sign in line is house 2 — no crooked slices.

So when the Moon is in Aries and you are Taurus, it sits in your **12th house** (the quiet, private sector).

*House list (short):* 1 self · 2 money · 3 talks/near road · 4 home · 5 play/love · 6 work · 7 partners · 8 shared depths · 9 distance/learning · 10 career · 11 friends/goals · 12 rest/secrets.
