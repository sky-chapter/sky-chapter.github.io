# Retrograde (R)

**Retrograde** means a planet *appears* to move backwards in the sky — an optical loop caused by orbital speeds. Astrologically it marks review: reread, repair, revisit.

Marking **R** after a planet (e.g., Saturn 8°15′ Aries R) tells you: take the brakes gently, finish old business, don't start new contracts with that theme if you can avoid it.
