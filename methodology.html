Method

Sky Chapter is built from real sky math, then translated into plain language. Every number below comes from an ephemeris, not from a horoscope column.

How a chapter is built

  • Ephemeris: JPL DE421 via skyfield; positions are computed for 00:00 UTC of each chapter date.
  • Zodiac: tropical (seasonal), ecliptic longitudes.
  • Houses: whole-sign solar, counted from the sign itself.
  • Orbs: 6° standard, 8° for the Sun and Moon; retrogrades are marked R.
  • Data before words: planets, degrees and aspects come first; the text only explains them.

What it is not

Not a prediction. Chapters are reflection and entertainment — not medical, financial or legal advice. The sky describes weather, not orders.

Cookies

The site uses a small number of cookies and similar storage:

  • Essential (always on): none that are optional — the site works with everything off.
  • Analytics (only after you press Accept): Google Analytics (measurement id shared across sok.lv) stores a first-party cookie to count visits and page views; we filter reports by hostname. You can choose Essential only, change your mind by clearing site data, or block the tag in your browser.

No advertising trackers, no sale of data. Consent is stored in your browser (localStorage, key sc_cookie_consent); the analytics tag is not loaded until you accept. If you make no choice, the compact notice hides itself after 12 seconds — that is not consent, analytics stays blocked. Reopen it any time with Cookie settings in the footer.
