--- layout: default title: "Method" permalink: /methodology/ --- # Method Sky Chapter is built from **real sky math**, then translated into plain language. - **Ephemeris:** JPL DE421 via `skyfield`; positions at **00:00 UTC** of each chapter date. - **Zodiac:** tropical (seasonal), ecliptic longitudes. - **Houses:** whole-sign solar, counted from the sign itself. - **Orbs:** 6° standard, 8° for Sun/Moon; retrogrades marked **R**. - **Data > words:** planets, degrees and aspects come first; the text only explains them. **Not a prediction.** Chapters are reflection and entertainment — not medical, financial or legal advice. The sky describes weather, not orders.
